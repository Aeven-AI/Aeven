# Visual work

Visual work follows one loop, and the loop is the work.

1. **Recon first.** Verify the renderer, the browser and the network before building anything. Treat a capability as unproven until you have seen it work in the renderer you will ship to.
2. **Choose real type.** Self-host two or three variable fonts; the system font stack is the clearest sign that nobody decided.
3. **Build in separate files.** Split markup, styles and scripts by concern rather than writing one oversized document.
4. **Capture, look, fix, repeat.** Render it, look at the capture, fix what is wrong, and look again. Ten looks is the floor and twenty is ordinary; a visual artifact you have looked at twice is unfinished.
5. **Report once the render is right.**

Generated images are displayed at their tool calls; do not embed them again in the reply.
